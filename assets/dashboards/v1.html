EMAIL_CORP drives 57.1% of all manual invoices (14,969 invoices) with only a 7.24% touchless rate — the lowest among active channels. EXCEL and PYMNTRQST have 0% touchless. Transitioning EMAIL vendors to ARIBA is the highest-leverage intervention.

Root Cause Summary
Structured diagnosis of processing failures
ROOT CAUSE 1 · Channel Dependency
48.8% of invoices come via EMAIL_CORP with only 7.24% touchless. Unstructured email submissions bypass automation logic.
ROOT CAUSE 2 · Missing Exception Data
98.66% of manual invoices lack exception codes. Without this data, automated routing and escalation rules cannot fire.
ROOT CAUSE 3 · Vendor Master Gaps
Several high-volume vendors show near-0% touchless despite ARIBA enrollment — suggesting invalid IDs, PO mismatches, or incomplete vendor master data.
ROOT CAUSE 4 · Posting Errors
PO posting errors account for 97% of documented exceptions. These stem from price/qty discrepancies and invalid requester IDs at invoice submission.

🔴 High Priority (Manual >500, <10% Touchless)

🟡 Medium Priority (Manual 200–500 or 10–30% Touchless)
Schedule structured onboarding review. Validate PO matching rules, payment terms in vendor master, and submission format compliance. Target improvement within 60 days.

Maintain performance. Monitor for regression. Use as benchmark for Team best-practice sharing with High/Medium priority vendors. Recognize in supplier scorecards.
